Privacy policy

Mariusz is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

With the read-only scope drive.metadata.readonly it reads the names, dates, sizes and media details of files in the owner's own Google Drive. It does not read file contents, change anything, or sell, share or transfer any Google user data. The data stays on the owner's computer.

Mariusz's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
